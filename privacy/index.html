How to use this page

  1. Read the information categories to understand what you may be asked to provide.
  2. Review why information is used, who may receive it and how long it may be kept.
  3. Use the rights section if you need access, correction or another privacy-related action.
  4. Send requests through the official Agrolyst SIWES contact channel and provide enough detail to identify your application.
  5. Never send passwords, verification codes or account recovery secrets in a privacy request.

1. Who this notice covers

This notice applies to students, applicants, placement participants and other individuals who interact with the Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services SIWES applicant portal. In this notice, “Agrolyst”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services.

2. Information we may collect

  • Identity details: name and other identifying details you provide.
  • Contact details: email address, telephone number and contact information.
  • Education details: institution, course, department, study level and SIWES dates.
  • Application records: answers, application status, correspondence and supporting documents you submit.
  • Placement information: attendance, progress, supervisor feedback and records relevant to an approved placement.
  • Account and security records: sign-in activity, verification status and technical logs needed to protect the portal.

Please provide only information relevant to your application and the stated purpose.

3. Why we use information

We use information to register applicants, manage accounts, assess eligibility, review application documents, communicate decisions and requests, coordinate placements, prepare relevant letters, support students, maintain records, prevent fraud and protect the portal. We may also use information to meet legal, institutional or programme administration requirements.

4. How information is obtained

Most information comes directly from you when you register, complete forms, upload documents, contact the programme or participate in SIWES activities. Where necessary and appropriate, relevant details may also be confirmed with your educational institution, an authorised placement supervisor or another source connected with your application.

5. Application documents and checks

Documents you submit may be examined to determine whether they are readable, complete and relevant to the stated requirement. We may record the outcome of a document check, request a clearer copy or ask you to explain an inconsistency. A document check is not a substitute for confirmation from the issuing institution. You may contact the programme to ask questions or request that a concern be reviewed by an authorised person.

6. When information may be shared

We do not sell personal information. Relevant information may be shared with authorised Agrolyst personnel who need it for application and placement administration; your institution or authorised SIWES coordinators where required; placement supervisors where relevant; professional advisers; and service providers who support the operation of the portal and communications. Information may also be disclosed where required by law, a lawful request or to protect the rights, safety and security of individuals and the programme.

7. Security and confidentiality

We apply reasonable organisational and technical safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, alteration or disclosure. Access should be limited to people who need information for their responsibilities. No online service can promise absolute security, so you should also protect your password, avoid shared-device sign-ins where possible and report suspected account misuse promptly.

8. Retention of information

We keep application and placement information for as long as reasonably necessary for application administration, placement records, student support, dispute handling, legal obligations and legitimate record-keeping needs. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of record and applicable institutional or legal requirements. When information is no longer required, it should be securely deleted, anonymised or otherwise disposed of in accordance with applicable procedures.

9. Your privacy choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to know whether we hold personal information about you, request access to it, correct inaccurate or incomplete details, request deletion or restriction where appropriate, object to certain processing, or raise a concern about how information is handled. You may also withdraw consent where a particular activity relies on consent. Some records may need to be retained where a legal or legitimate administrative requirement applies.

10. How to make a request

Contact Agrolyst through the official SIWES programme contact channel shown on the portal. Explain the request and provide the minimum information needed to locate your record. We may need to verify your identity before acting. We aim to respond within the time required by applicable law. Do not send passwords or one-time verification codes.

11. Cookies and essential browser functions

The portal may use essential browser storage or similar technologies to keep your session secure, remember necessary preferences and support core functions. Disabling these features may affect sign-in or other portal functions.

12. Student information and third parties

If you provide information about another person, you should have authority to do so and should inform them where appropriate. Do not upload another student's records or unrelated personal information. If you believe information has been submitted incorrectly, contact the programme so it can be assessed.

13. Processing outside Nigeria

Some service providers supporting the portal may process information in locations outside Nigeria. Where personal information is transferred internationally, appropriate safeguards and requirements under applicable Nigerian data protection law should be observed.

14. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when the portal, programme procedures or applicable requirements change. The effective date displayed at the top identifies the current version. Please review the page periodically.

15. Contact

For questions, concerns or requests about personal information, contact Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services through the official contact channel published for the SIWES programme or the portal home page. Please include enough information to help us understand your request without exposing your account secrets.

Need assistance?
For questions about your SIWES application or the information on this page, use the official contact details published by Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services. Never share your password or one-time sign-in code with anyone.
