Privacy Notice

This notice explains how Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services handles information submitted through its SIWES applicant portal.

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services (“Agrolyst”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects the privacy of students and other people who use the SIWES applicant portal. This notice describes the information we may collect, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information we collect

Depending on the application process and the features you use, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact details, such as your full name, email address, telephone number and contact information you choose to provide.
  • School and placement information, such as your institution, course of study, department, SIWES dates and relevant application details.
  • Application documents and information you submit, including supporting documents and correspondence relating to your placement.
  • Account and technical information, such as authentication status, basic log information, and information needed to keep the portal secure.

If you choose Google sign-in, Google and our authentication provider process the information needed to authenticate your account. We receive account details made available through that sign-in process.

2. How we use your information

We use the information to create and maintain your account, assess and administer SIWES applications, communicate about your application, prepare relevant placement or acceptance documents, provide support, prevent misuse, protect the portal, and meet applicable legal or administrative obligations.

How to use this privacy notice

  1. Read the information categories to see what you may submit through the portal.
  2. Review the sections about AI-assisted document review, service providers, storage and retention.
  3. Use the rights section if you want to access, correct, object to, restrict or request deletion of personal information, or challenge an automated result.
  4. Make a request through the official SIWES programme contact channel. Do not include your password, one-time codes or other login secrets.
  5. Review this notice again when it is updated; the effective date above identifies the current version.

3. Legal basis and consent

Where applicable law requires a legal basis for processing, we process information on a basis appropriate to the purpose, such as taking steps at your request, administering a placement, complying with a legal obligation, protecting legitimate operational interests, or obtaining your consent where consent is required. You may withdraw consent where processing relies on consent, although this does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with authorised Agrolyst personnel and, where necessary for SIWES administration, your institution or placement contacts. We also use service providers to operate the portal. These include Supabase for database, account authentication and private document storage; Google Gemini AI for automated analysis of uploaded SIWES documents; and an SMTP email delivery provider for account and application messages. The email provider is selected through the portal's configured mail settings. Providers receive only information needed to perform their services and are expected to apply appropriate contractual and security safeguards. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect people, rights and the security of the service.

5. AI-assisted document review and automated outcomes

When you upload a required SIWES document, the portal may securely send the file and relevant application context (such as your name, institution, department, course and study level) to Google Gemini for document analysis. The AI checks matters such as the apparent document type, legibility, presence of expected information and consistency with the application details. The review result, findings and a file-integrity hash may be stored with your application record. The AI is not able to conclusively authenticate a document, verify an issuing authority's records or guarantee that a document is genuine. Automated review rules may accept or reject a document or update an application status where specified confidence and completeness checks are met; other cases may be returned for correction or flagged for further review. AI results can be inaccurate. You may request human intervention, explain your position and challenge an automated outcome through the official SIWES programme contact channel. Do not upload documents that are unrelated to your SIWES application.

6. Storage and security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, alteration or disclosure. No online service or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your password confidential and contact us promptly if you suspect unauthorised use of your account.

7. Retention

We retain account, application, document, review and communication records only for as long as reasonably necessary for the SIWES application and placement process, programme administration, security, dispute handling and applicable legal obligations. The precise retention period depends on the record type and the circumstances. Records may be retained longer where required by law or needed to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, and securely deleted or de-identified when no longer required. You may contact us to ask about the retention period applicable to your information.

8. Your choices and rights

Subject to the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other applicable law, you may have rights to be informed, request access to your personal data, request correction or erasure, object to or request restriction of processing, request portability where applicable, withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, and complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. You may also request human intervention and challenge a decision based solely on automated processing where the law provides that right. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate administrative or legal reasons. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request. We will respond within the period required by applicable law.

9. Cookies and account security

The portal and its authentication services may use essential browser storage or similar technologies to maintain a signed-in session, protect accounts and support core functions. Blocking these technologies may prevent some features from working properly.

10. Children and student information

The portal is intended for people applying for or participating in SIWES and related placements. If you submit information on behalf of another person, make sure you are authorised to do so. If you believe information has been submitted improperly, contact us so we can review it.

11. International processing

Some service providers may process or store information in locations outside Nigeria. Where personal data is transferred internationally, Agrolyst should use the transfer safeguards and legal mechanisms required by applicable Nigerian data protection law. Contact us through the official SIWES programme channel if you need more information about the relevant safeguards.

12. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the portal, our practices or applicable requirements change. The effective date above indicates when this version was published. Please review this page periodically.

13. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests relating to your information, contact Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services through the official contact channel provided by the SIWES programme or on the portal home page. Include enough detail for us to understand your request, but do not send passwords or other authentication secrets.

Please note: This notice describes the intended handling of portal information. Actual practices should be checked against the services and processes currently used by Agrolyst.
