How to use this page

  1. Check the current SIWES intake instructions before you begin.
  2. Use your own account and enter accurate student information.
  3. Prepare clear, complete documents in the requested format before uploading.
  4. Check your dashboard and registered email for application updates.
  5. Follow the correction instructions if a document or application needs attention.

1. Purpose and scope

This policy explains the day-to-day rules for using the Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services SIWES applicant portal. It applies to applicants and students who create accounts, submit documents, check application progress or communicate with the programme through the portal.

2. Before you apply

Read the current intake information and confirm that the opportunity is suitable for your institution, course and SIWES period. Prepare the required details and documents before starting. Application requirements may vary by intake, so follow the instructions shown on the portal and in official programme communications.

3. Account rules

  • Create and use one account for yourself unless the programme instructs otherwise.
  • Use an email address that you can access.
  • Keep your password and verification codes private.
  • Do not share accounts or impersonate another student.
  • Update contact information when it changes and report suspicious access.

4. Document submission standards

Upload clear, complete and readable files in the format and size requested by the portal. Ensure that names and key details are visible and consistent across your application. Do not submit cropped pages that remove important information, password-protected files unless requested, unrelated documents, forged records or documents belonging to another person. Keep the original documents and a copy of every file you submit.

5. Application status and corrections

Check your dashboard for the latest status and any instructions. If a document is marked as needing attention, read the reason carefully, prepare the corrected document and follow the resubmission instructions. A submitted application is not necessarily an approved application. Do not repeatedly submit duplicate applications to try to change a decision; contact the programme if you believe a status is incorrect.

6. Communication standards

Use the official contact channel for application questions. Include your full name and relevant application reference where available, and describe the issue clearly. Be respectful and avoid sending repeated messages about the same unresolved issue. Never send your password or one-time code to anyone claiming to provide support.

7. Prohibited activity

  • Submitting false information or altered documents.
  • Attempting to access another applicant's account or records.
  • Sharing private applicant information without permission.
  • Uploading malware, abusive content or unrelated material.
  • Interfering with portal availability, security or normal operation.
  • Using the portal for fraud, harassment or unlawful activity.

8. Accessibility and technical difficulties

If a page does not load, a file will not upload or you encounter another technical problem, check your internet connection, confirm that the file meets the displayed requirements and try again. If the issue continues, contact the programme with the page name, approximate time and a short description. Do not include passwords or verification codes in screenshots or support messages.

9. Fair and respectful treatment

Applicants should be treated respectfully and should communicate respectfully with programme personnel and other students. Discrimination, harassment, threats and abusive conduct are not acceptable. Concerns about unfair treatment or inappropriate conduct may be raised through the official programme contact channel.

10. Privacy and data handling

Use of personal information is explained in the Privacy Notice. Submit only information needed for the SIWES process and avoid sharing another person's private details. The Participation Agreement sets out additional terms for portal use and placement participation.

11. Policy updates and enforcement

Agrolyst may update this policy to reflect programme requirements, portal improvements or applicable law. Serious or repeated breaches may lead to a request for correction, restriction of portal access or further programme action, subject to applicable law and any relevant review process. The current version is the one published on this page.

Need assistance?
For questions about your SIWES application or the information on this page, use the official contact details published by Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services. Never share your password or one-time sign-in code with anyone.
