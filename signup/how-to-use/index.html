ACCOUNT SETUP GUIDE

How to create and activate your account

Follow these steps to register for the Agrolyst SIWES Applicant Portal, confirm your email address and open your dashboard. Use the same email address that you entered on your SIWES application.

Already submitted an application? Start with the same email address used on your application. If it is already registered to an application, follow the portal's link-existing-application process instead of creating a duplicate.
Step 1

Open the Create Account page

Visit the signup page and check that you are on the Agrolyst SIWES portal. You should see the email address and password fields.

Step 2

Enter your details and create your account

Enter an email address you can access and create a password. Check the spelling of your email before submitting the form.

Step 3

Open the account activation email

Check your inbox for the Agrolyst account confirmation message. If it is not visible, check your spam or junk folder. Use the confirmation link only for your own account.

Step 4

Follow the confirmation link

Open the confirmation message and select its account confirmation link. Complete the confirmation in the browser. Do not share the link with anyone.

If an error appears

What to do if the email link shows an error

This is an issue to resolve, not a successful signup step. Return to the portal and try the current confirmation flow. If the error continues, contact Agrolyst support and include a screenshot, but hide private tokens and personal information.

Step 5

Confirm that your dashboard opens

After successful confirmation, sign in if the portal asks you to. Your applicant dashboard is where you can check your application and follow the instructions displayed there.
