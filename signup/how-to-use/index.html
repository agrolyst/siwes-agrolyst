Your account setup walkthrough

Create your Agrolyst SIWES account with confidence.

This guide takes you through the signup process from the first page to your applicant dashboard. Follow the instructions in order, read each note carefully and use the screenshots to recognise the page or email you should be seeing.

ℹ️
Before you begin

Keep access to your email inbox. Use the email address you supplied with your SIWES application, enter it carefully and choose a password you can remember. Never send your password or a private confirmation link to another person.

FOLLOW EACH STEP IN ORDER

Signup and account activation

01 First, open signup

Go to the official Create Account page

Open the Agrolyst SIWES portal in your browser and select the Create Account option, or use the button below. Check the page heading and Agrolyst branding before entering any information.

  • Use the official portal address: siwes.agrolystfarms.com/signup/.
  • Make sure the page has loaded completely before typing.
  • If you already have an account, use Sign in instead of registering again.
Important: If you have submitted an SIWES application before, use the same email address used on that application. The portal may direct you to link your existing application rather than create a duplicate.
02 Enter your details

Provide your email address and create a password

The signup form asks for two important details. Complete both fields before selecting Create account.

  • Email address: Enter an email account that belongs to you and that you can open now. Check every letter, number and the part after the @ sign.
  • Password: Create a password with at least six characters. Choose one that is difficult for another person to guess.
  • Use the Show password option to check what you typed, then hide it again before leaving the page.
  • Read the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy links if you need to understand the portal conditions.

When both fields are complete, select Create account once and wait while the portal checks your application details and processes the registration. Avoid repeatedly clicking the button.

Prevent common mistakes: Do not add spaces before or after your email. Do not use an email address with a spelling error, and do not enter your email password in the portal's password field.
03 Check your inbox

Find the account confirmation email

After the signup form accepts your details, the portal may display a message asking you to confirm your email address. Open the inbox for the exact email address you entered on the signup page.

  • Look for the account confirmation email from Agrolyst.
  • If you do not see it, refresh your inbox and check Spam, Junk, Promotions or Updates folders.
  • Check that you are looking in the correct email account.
  • Open the message and read the instructions before selecting its confirmation link.
Keep your account safe: Do not forward the confirmation email. Do not post its confirmation link or code in a screenshot or public message.
04 Confirm your email

Open the confirmation link carefully

Inside the confirmation email, select the button or link provided to confirm your email address. Allow the page to open fully in your browser. Do not copy the link into an unfamiliar website.

  • Use the same browser where you can continue to the Agrolyst portal.
  • Wait for the confirmation page to finish loading.
  • If the portal asks you to sign in after confirmation, return to the official Sign in page and use your registered email and portal password.
  • If the link says it has expired or cannot be used, do not keep reusing that same link. Return to the portal and follow the available account confirmation or support instructions.
Do not close the page too quickly. Wait for a clear confirmation result or for the next page to load.
! Troubleshooting

If Gmail opens a page showing an error

An error page is not proof that your email has been confirmed. Follow these checks before trying again.

  • Check that the address bar shows the official Agrolyst SIWES website.
  • Make sure the confirmation link has not been cut off or altered.
  • If the link has expired or was already used, return to the portal and request or follow the current confirmation instructions.
  • Try opening the email link in your normal browser, not inside an unfamiliar in-app browser.
  • If the error continues, take a screenshot of the message and contact the portal support team for assistance.
Protect your details: Before sharing an error screenshot, hide your email address, private confirmation link, codes and any long token shown in the browser address.
05 Finish account setup

Open your applicant dashboard

When your email confirmation is successful, continue to the Agrolyst SIWES portal. If you are not taken to your dashboard automatically, open the Sign in page and enter the email address and password you registered with.

  • Wait for the dashboard to load before refreshing or closing the tab.
  • Check that you are signed in to the correct account.
  • Read the notices and instructions displayed on your dashboard.
  • Use the dashboard links to continue with your SIWES application, documents and any outstanding requirements.
Remember: Creating a portal account does not by itself mean that your SIWES application has been approved. Follow the application and document instructions shown in your account.

Before you finish

Use this quick checklist to make sure you have completed the account setup carefully.

  • I used the official Agrolyst portal.
  • I entered the correct email address.
  • I created a password of at least six characters.
  • I checked my inbox and spam folder.
  • I opened the confirmation link safely.
  • I saw the confirmation result.
  • I signed in and reached my dashboard.
  • I read the next instructions on the dashboard.
