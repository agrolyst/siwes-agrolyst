How to use the SIWES portal

Follow this guide to submit your application, create or access your account, connect an application you have already submitted, and manage your documents through the Agrolyst SIWES Applicant Portal.

Already submitted an application? Do not submit another application with the same email. Choose Sign up and enter the email address you used before. The portal will direct you to securely link your existing application.

1. How to apply for SIWES

01

Open the public application website

Visit applysiwes.agrolystfarms.com and read the application instructions before starting.

02

Complete the application form

Enter your personal, school and SIWES details carefully. Use an email address you can access because it will be used for application communication and account linking.

03

Review and submit

Check your entries and confirm the required declarations before submitting. Save your application reference and keep access to the email address you supplied.

04

Wait for application review

The published estimate is approximately 20–30 minutes for most complete applications. Allow up to 24 hours during busy periods or when clarification is needed. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed decision time.

2. How to create your portal account

  1. Open the Create Account page.
  2. Enter your email address and choose a strong password.
  3. The portal checks whether that email has already been used for a SIWES application.
  4. If it is a new application email, continue with account creation and follow the email confirmation instructions.
  5. After confirmation, sign in to access your applicant dashboard.

Important: Use the same email address you used on the public application form. A different email may not match your application.

3. If you have already applied before

If you enter an email address that has already been used for an application, the signup process will redirect you to the application-link page instead of creating a duplicate application account.

  1. Confirm that the email shown on the page is the email you used when applying.
  2. Request the six-digit verification code.
  3. Check that email inbox and its spam or junk folder.
  4. Enter the code on the application-link page before it expires.
  5. After successful verification, the portal will connect the eligible application and open your dashboard.

Never share your verification code with anyone. If the email address is not yours, do not continue. If a record cannot be linked or multiple records need attention, contact Agrolyst through its official support channel.

4. How to sign in

  1. Open the Sign in page.
  2. Enter the email address and password registered to your portal account.
  3. Complete the email verification-code step when prompted.
  4. Enter the code sent to your email to finish signing in.
  5. Once verified, you will be taken to your applicant dashboard.

5. Using your applicant dashboard

Your dashboard is the main place to follow your application and use the available applicant services. Sign in first, then:

Application status

Check the status and any instructions displayed for your application.

Documents

Open the document area to upload or review the documents requested for your application.

Compliance

Review outstanding requirements and follow the instructions shown for any item that needs attention.

Acceptance letter

Where an acceptance letter has been issued and made available to your account, open the acceptance-letter section to view it.

6. Uploading documents

  1. Sign in to your account.
  2. Open the Documents section from your dashboard.
  3. Choose the document type requested on the page.
  4. Select a clear, complete and readable file that belongs to you.
  5. Submit the upload and wait for the page to confirm the result.
  6. Return later to check whether the document is pending, accepted or requires changes.

Do not upload blank, altered, misleading or unrelated files. If a document is marked as requiring changes, read the reason shown and upload a corrected version.

7. Password help

If you cannot remember your password, use the Forgot Password page and follow the email instructions. If you receive a password-reset link, open it and set a new password. Do not share reset links or verification codes.

9. Common questions

I have already applied. Should I apply again?

No. Use the same email address on the signup page. If it matches an existing application, you will be directed to link it to your account.

I did not receive the verification code. What should I do?

Check your spam or junk folder, confirm that the email address is correct, and request a new code from the page. If the issue continues, use the official Agrolyst support channel.

Can I use a different email from the one on my application?

Use the original application email when linking your application. If you no longer have access to it, contact Agrolyst support so the record can be checked safely.

Does the review estimate guarantee approval?

No. The 20–30 minute estimate, with up to 24 hours allowed during busy periods or clarification, refers to an expected review timeframe only. It does not guarantee acceptance or approval.

What if I see a message that my application cannot be linked?

Do not create duplicate records or repeatedly submit the form. Contact Agrolyst through its official support channel and provide your application reference if you have one.
