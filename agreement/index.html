1. About Agrolyst and the SIWES programme

Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services provides an applicant portal to support the administration of Student Industrial Work Experience Scheme (SIWES) applications and related placement communication. The programme is intended to help eligible students understand application requirements, submit relevant information, receive updates and, where selected, take part in approved practical learning activities.

SIWES is an educational and practical training experience. It is not automatically an offer of permanent employment, a guarantee of a particular placement, a guaranteed stipend or an assurance that every applicant will be accepted.

2. Eligibility and application

Applicants must meet the eligibility and documentation requirements communicated for the relevant intake. You are responsible for checking that your institution, course, study level, training period and other details are correct. Submission of an application means that you are asking to be considered; it does not mean that your application has been approved.

3. Accurate information and genuine documents

  • Provide truthful, complete and up-to-date information.
  • Submit documents that belong to you or that you are authorised to provide.
  • Do not forge, alter, misrepresent or knowingly submit misleading documents.
  • Respond promptly where clarification or a corrected document is requested.
  • Tell the programme if any important information changes after submission.

Agrolyst may request clarification or further evidence where a submission is incomplete, unclear or inconsistent.

4. Account security and responsible access

Keep your password, verification codes and account recovery information private. Use an email address you control, sign out on shared devices and contact the programme if you suspect unauthorised access. You must not impersonate another applicant, access another person's account, interfere with portal operations or attempt to bypass security restrictions.

5. Student conduct and workplace expectations

Where a placement is offered, students are expected to act respectfully, honestly and professionally. Follow reasonable instructions from authorised supervisors, observe workplace safety rules, use equipment responsibly, respect colleagues and members of the public, and avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, violence, theft or deliberate damage. Any site-specific handbook or safety instruction supplied for a placement must also be followed.

6. Attendance, punctuality and reporting

Students should follow the attendance schedule and reporting arrangements communicated for their placement. Notify the designated supervisor as early as reasonably possible if illness, an emergency or another genuine circumstance affects attendance. Do not falsify attendance records, timesheets, logbooks or supervisor comments. Institution-specific reporting requirements remain the student's responsibility.

7. Confidentiality and responsible use of information

Do not disclose confidential business, operational, staff, customer, farm, supplier or student information obtained during the application or placement. Do not photograph, copy, publish or share restricted records, facilities or activities without permission. This obligation does not prevent disclosures that are protected or required by law.

8. Application assessment and communication

Applications may be checked for completeness, relevance and consistency with the stated requirements. An application may be marked as received, under review, awaiting correction, accepted or declined, as applicable. Applicants should rely on official portal updates and written communication rather than informal promises. Agrolyst may contact applicants to request additional information or explain next steps.

9. Placement, supervision and learning outcomes

Any placement is subject to availability, suitability, programme needs and the written conditions communicated to the student. The activities, location, schedule, supervision arrangements and learning objectives may differ by placement. Students should keep their institution's required logbook and reports current and seek guidance from their academic supervisor where necessary. Completion records or confirmation are subject to attendance, conduct and the applicable programme requirements.

10. Allowances, expenses and employment status

No stipend, allowance, transport support, accommodation, insurance benefit or other payment is promised by this general agreement. Any such arrangement must be expressly confirmed in writing by an authorised Agrolyst representative. SIWES participation does not by itself create permanent employment or an employment relationship.

11. Health, safety and property

Observe all safety briefings, protective-equipment requirements, hygiene rules and site instructions. Report hazards, injuries, near misses, damaged equipment or unsafe conditions promptly to the designated supervisor. Do not operate machinery, vehicles, tools or chemicals unless authorised and properly instructed. Students must take reasonable care of property entrusted to them.

12. Changes, suspension and withdrawal

Agrolyst may update portal procedures or placement arrangements where operational, educational or legal circumstances require it. Access or participation may be restricted where there is a serious breach of these terms, a safety concern, suspected fraud or another reasonable programme need. Where appropriate, the student will be informed of the concern and any available next steps. Students may withdraw by notifying the programme through its official contact channel.

13. Privacy and related documents

Personal information is handled as explained in the Privacy Notice. The Portal Policy provides additional practical rules for account use, submissions and communications. These documents should be read together.

14. Limits and applicable law

This agreement does not remove any right or protection that cannot lawfully be excluded. It is intended to operate subject to the applicable laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria and any mandatory requirements of the student's institution or SIWES programme. Where a separate written placement letter or institution-approved arrangement sets additional conditions, those specific conditions should be read alongside this agreement.

15. Acceptance and updates

By registering for or continuing to use the portal, you acknowledge that you have had an opportunity to read this agreement and agree to follow the terms that apply to your use and participation. The effective date at the top identifies this version. Agrolyst may revise the agreement and publish a new effective date when programme requirements or portal procedures change.

Need assistance?
For questions about your SIWES application or the information on this page, use the official contact details published by Agrolyst Integrated Farms and Services. Never share your password or one-time sign-in code with anyone.
